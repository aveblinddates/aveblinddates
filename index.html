QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How does Ave Maria Blind Dating work?

You complete the quiz with honest answers about yourself, your interests, personality, values, lifestyle, and what you're looking for. Participants who submit during the month are considered together when the monthly matching cycle takes place.

When are matches made?

Matches are made once a month, on the first day of the month. The goal is to consider the people in that month's pool together rather than creating pairs automatically every time someone signs up.

Do I have to sign up every month?

Yes. Each monthly dating pool is separate. After the matching cycle, the previous pool is cleared. If you'd like to participate again during a new month, you'll need to submit the quiz again.

Can I submit the quiz more than once in the same month?

No. Each participant may submit one application per monthly dating pool.

Am I guaranteed a match?

No. A match cannot be guaranteed because we don't know how many eligible students will participate each month. The available dating pool will depend on who signs up.

If you'd like to increase the number of potential matches, share the site with other eligible students and encourage them to participate.

Who can participate?

The site is intended for students who are 18 or older and currently attend Ave Maria University.

Who will I be matched with?

The matching process is intended for heterosexual dating relationships. Participants will therefore be matched with an eligible student of the opposite sex when a compatible pairing is available.

What information is used to make matches?

The matching process considers responses intended to describe compatibility, such as personality, interests, lifestyle, communication preferences, values, relationship goals, and other relevant answers from the quiz.

Are the "Deal Breakers & Icks" used to match people?

No. The dealbreaker section is separate from the matching criteria. Those answers are intended for internal, anonymous trend research and future dating resources rather than deciding who should be matched with whom.

Who can see my information?

Participant information is kept private and is accessible only to the person operating the matching system. Your responses are not displayed publicly.

How will I find out if I have a match?

If you are matched, an email will be sent to both participants. The email will contain information about the match and additional instructions for setting up your blind date.

Will my match know my information before the date?

The goal of the experience is to preserve the mystery while still giving both people enough information to comfortably arrange their date. Specific information shared with a match will be explained in the match email.

What happens after my blind date?

A future post-date feedback form will give participants an opportunity to tell us how the experience went, whether they would be interested in a second date, and how the experience could be improved.

Will reviews appear on the website?

Participants will eventually be able to submit reviews of their experience. Reviews will be manually reviewed and verified before being publicly displayed.

How will the dating statistics be collected?

The dealbreaker and "ick" questions are intended to help identify anonymous trends. These statistics may be used to create future resources about dating, communication, relationships, and common student preferences.

What if I want my information removed?

Email ave.blinddates@gmail.com and request that your information be removed.

Is Ave Maria Blind Dating affiliated with Ave Maria University?

No. Ave Maria Blind Dating is an independent student-created project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or administered by Ave Maria University.
