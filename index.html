QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ave Maria Blind Dating?

Ave Maria Blind Dating is an independent student project designed to help Ave Maria University students meet someone based on personality, interests, values, and compatibility rather than appearance.

Who can participate?

Any Ave Maria University student who is 18 years of age or older may participate.

How does the matching process work?

Students complete the questionnaire and enter the current month's pool. Matches are created once a month based on compatibility between participants.

When are matches made?

Matches are made on the first of each month. Participants should submit their questionnaire before the end of the previous month to be considered for that month's matching cycle.

What happens if I submit after the 1st?

Your submission will be placed in the next month's pool. Matching does not happen immediately after you submit.

Will I definitely get a match?

No. A match cannot be guaranteed because the number of participants, age compatibility, and the number of students participating in each gender can vary from month to month.

Want to increase the chances of getting a blind date? Share the site with other Ave Maria students and encourage them to participate.

How are matches chosen?

Matching will consider responses such as personality, interests, communication style, lifestyle, values, relationship goals, and age compatibility.

Are matches based on appearance?

No. The purpose of the experience is to get to know someone based on who they are rather than starting with a photograph or profile picture.

Are the dealbreaker questions used for matching?

No. The dealbreaker and "ick" questions are collected separately for anonymous trend data and future dating resources. They are not used to determine someone's match.

How often can I participate?

You may submit one entry during each monthly matching cycle. At the end of each cycle, the current pool is cleared. If you want to participate again, you'll need to submit the questionnaire again for the new month.

Who can see my answers?

Your individual responses are kept private and are only accessible to the person running Ave Maria Blind Dating. Your answers are not publicly displayed.

Will my email address be given to my match?

No. Your personal email address will not be given directly to your match. If you are matched, both participants will receive an email explaining the next steps for arranging their blind date.

What if I change my mind?

Email ave.blinddates@gmail.com and request to be removed from the current month's matching pool.

Does it cost anything?

No. Participating in Ave Maria Blind Dating is completely free.

What happens after I get a match?

You'll receive an email explaining that you've been matched and providing more information about how to set up your blind date.

Am I required to go on the date?

No. Receiving a match is an opportunity to meet someone, not an obligation to go on a date.

What if my match and I don't click?

That's okay. A blind date isn't a guarantee of a relationship. The goal is simply to give you an opportunity to meet someone new.

Is Ave Maria Blind Dating affiliated with Ave Maria University?

No. Ave Maria Blind Dating is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially associated with Ave Maria University.

What happens to the monthly pool?

The current matching pool is cleared after each monthly matching cycle. If you want to participate in another month, you will need to submit a new questionnaire.

Ready to take a chance?

You don't know who your match will be. That's the point.
