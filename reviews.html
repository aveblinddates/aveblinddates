REAL EXPERIENCES

Everyone's experience is different.

A blind date might lead to a great conversation, an unexpected connection, a friendship, or simply a memorable evening. There isn't one way for the experience to turn out.

As the program grows, this page will feature reviews submitted by previous participants.

PRIVACY

Your story stays yours.

Reviews are not automatically published. Submitted experiences will be manually reviewed before they appear on the website.

Personal information should not be included in a public review unless you are comfortable with it being displayed.

The goal is to share useful experiences while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.
